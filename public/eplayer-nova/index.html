☁️ Google Drive Cloud Synchronization

EPlayer Nova provides optional personal cloud backup and multi-device synchronization powered by Google Drive. When enabled by the user, the app synchronizes your musical organization across Android and desktop devices.

Your privacy is our foremost priority. Here is how EPlayer Nova uses Google Drive:

  • Isolated App Data Storage Sync files are stored exclusively in your Google Drive Application Data folder (spaces=appDataFolder), hidden from third parties and not mixed with your documents.
  • Strict Scope Boundaries The app only requests drive.appdata and drive.file. It cannot view, modify, or access any other files in your Google Drive.
  • No Data Sharing Your metadata is never sent to any developer-owned or third-party servers. Data flows strictly between your device and Google's official Drive API.
  • User Controlled You can disconnect Google Drive and revoke permissions at any moment directly from the application settings or your Google Account security page.
